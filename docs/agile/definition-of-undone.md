# Definition of Undone

Definition of Undone is an optional local aid, not a required Scrum artifact. Use it to make incomplete work and its consequences visible rather than mistaking activity or partial implementation for a finished outcome.

Work is Undone if any applicable Definition of Done criterion is unmet, including when acceptance criteria remain incomplete, required checks fail or have not run, or required documentation or verification is missing.

When work is Undone:

- Keep its status visibly in progress or return it to the backlog; do not report it as Done.
- Record what is complete, what remains, test results, and known risks in the work item or pull request.
- Keep incomplete changes out of the completed product unless the team has deliberately agreed on a safe, separately verified way to integrate them.
- Reassess remaining scope, dependencies, and readiness before continuing or splitting the work.

If the work cannot be completed as planned, communicate the impact and agree where the remaining work belongs. This definition does not prescribe a sprint or iteration rollover because no cadence is established for Mimir.
