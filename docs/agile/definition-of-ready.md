# Definition of Ready

This checklist is a proposed local aid for deciding whether a backlog item is clear enough to start. A Definition of Ready is not a Scrum requirement. Use judgment: a small change does not need ceremony or detail that adds no value.

An item is ready to start when the applicable points below are true:

- The intended user or technical outcome is understandable.
- Scope and acceptance criteria make the expected result observable and checkable. For defects, the current and expected behavior are clear.
- Important dependencies, affected areas, and known constraints are identified.
- Relevant data, security, and compatibility considerations have been considered; unresolved risks are visible.
- The work can be approached without a blocking unanswered question. It is small enough to make progress and verify, or has been split into clearer outcomes.
- The likely verification is understood, including which existing tests or checks apply and whether new tests or documentation are needed.

If a blocking point is missing, clarify the item or record the question and its owner in the team's usual work-tracking location before starting. This checklist does not require speculative implementation details or answers to questions that do not affect the work.
